Fix and flip deals don't wait. Whether you're buying at auction, off-market, or through the MLS, you need capital that moves at the pace of your deal — not the pace of a bank.

Our fix & flip loans fund the acquisition and up to 100% of rehab costs, so you can execute without tying up your own capital. We underwrite based on the asset: ARV, location, and your exit strategy. No W-2s, no tax returns, no bureaucratic delays.

From single-family flips to multi-unit value-add plays, North Star Capital gives you the capital partner you need to move confidently and close with certainty.
