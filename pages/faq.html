Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

What is a hard money loan? +

A hard money loan is a short-term, asset-based loan secured by real estate. Unlike traditional bank loans, hard money lenders focus primarily on the property's value and the deal's viability rather than the borrower's credit score or income documentation.

How is North Star Capital different from banks? +

We're faster, more flexible, and asset-based. Banks can take 30-60 days and often require extensive documentation. We can close in 5-10 business days and focus on deal fundamentals—property value, exit strategy, and borrower experience.

What states do you lend in? +

We lend nationwide with particular strength in the Southwest (AZ, NM, NV, TX), Midwest (OH, MI, IN, IL), Southeast (GA, TN, Carolinas, FL), and Pacific Northwest (WA, OR).

Loan Programs

What loan programs do you offer? +
  • Fix & Flip: Short-term financing for acquisitions and rehabs (6-12 months)
  • BRRRR / Rental: Bridge loans for rental property acquisitions (6-24 months)
  • Construction / Ground-Up: Financing for new builds and major rehabs
What are your typical LTV/LTC ratios? +

It varies by program:

  • Fix & Flip: Up to 75-90% LTV
  • Rental: Up to 75% LTV
  • Construction: Up to 80-90% LTC (land up to 50%)
What loan sizes do you do? +

Our typical loan ranges: $75K–$5M for fix and flip, $100K–$5M for rental, and $200K–$10M for construction. Larger deals considered on a case-by-case basis.

Rates & Terms

What are your interest rates? +

Rates start at:

  • Fix & Flip: 9-12%
  • Rental/BRRRR: 8-11%
  • Construction: 10-13%

Final rates depend on deal specifics, LTV, borrower experience, and property condition.

What are your points/fees? +

Typical points range from 2-3 points on the loan amount. Additional standard closing costs include title, appraisal, and legal fees. No hidden administrative fees.

What are your loan terms? +

Terms vary by program: 6-12 months for fix and flip, 6-24 months for rental, and 6-18 months for construction. Extensions may be available with additional fees.

The Process

How fast can I get funded? +

Submit your scenario → Get indicative terms within 24 hours → Underwrite and close in 5-10 business days. Timing depends on how quickly you provide required documentation.

What do I need to apply? +
  • Completed scenario form
  • Property address and details
  • Purchase contract (if under contract)
  • Scope of work / rehab budget
  • ARV estimates / comps
  • Entity documents (if applicable)
  • Photo documentation (for existing properties)
Do you require an appraisal? +

Yes, we typically require an appraisal for most transactions. For smaller deals or repeat borrowers, we may accept BPOs (Broker Price Opinions) or interior-only appraisals to reduce costs.

Borrower Requirements

What's the minimum credit score? +

We don't have a strict minimum credit score. We focus on the deal—property value, LTV, and exit strategy. However, better credit may result in better terms.

Do I need prior experience? +

We prefer borrowers with at least 2-3 completed deals, but we review each deal individually. Strong deals from newer investors may be considered with additional documentation or a more experienced partner.

Can I use an LLC or entity? +

Yes, we work with LLCs, corporations, and other entities. You'll need to provide formation documents and have authorized signatories ready for closing.
