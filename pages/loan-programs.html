Fix & Flip

Close fast, renovate, resell. Repeat.

Our fix & flip loans are built for speed. Whether you're buying at auction, off-market, or through the MLS, we move at your pace. We fund the acquisition and up to 100% of rehab costs, so you can execute the deal without tying up your own capital.

We focus on the asset — ARV, location, and your exit — not your credit score or W-2s.

Loan Terms

  • Purchase LTV Up to 90% of purchase price
  • Rehab Financing Up to 100% of rehab costs
  • Max LTC Up to 90% of total cost
  • Max ARV Up to 75% of ARV
  • Term 6–12 months
  • Interest Rate 9–12% (based on deal & experience)
  • Origination 2–3 points
  • Loan Size $75,000 – $5,000,000
  • Closing Time 5–10 business days

Ideal For

Single-family flips 2–4 unit properties Distressed acquisitions Auction purchases Off-market deals Value-add renovations

What We Look At

  • After Repair Value (ARV) Primary underwriting driver
  • Rehab budget Detailed scope required
  • Exit strategy Resale or refi
  • Borrower experience Track record helps terms

Landlords / BRRRR / DSCR

Acquire, stabilize, and hold — or refinance and repeat.

Our rental bridge loans are designed for buy-and-hold investors executing the BRRRR strategy and landlords acquiring stabilized or transitional rental properties. We bridge the gap between purchase and long-term financing.

DSCR underwriting means we qualify the deal on cash flow — not your personal income.

Loan Terms

  • LTV Up to 75%
  • DSCR 1.0x minimum preferred
  • Term 6–24 months
  • Interest Rate 8–11%
  • Origination 2–3 points
  • Loan Size $100,000 – $5,000,000
  • Occupancy Vacant or tenanted accepted
  • Closing Time 5–10 business days

Ideal For

BRRRR strategy Long-term rental holds Portfolio acquisitions Stabilization bridge Multi-family 2–8 units Lease-up period

What We Look At

  • As-is or stabilized value Primary collateral basis
  • Market rents Used for DSCR calculation
  • Exit strategy Refi to conventional/DSCR
  • Property condition Rentable or near-rentable

Developers / Ground-Up Construction

From dirt to certificate of occupancy — we fund the build.

Our ground-up construction loans are structured for experienced developers building single-family homes, small multifamily, and infill projects. Funds are disbursed in draws as construction milestones are completed and inspected.

We leverage land equity and fund construction costs so you can maximize your capital efficiency on every project.

Loan Terms

  • LTC Up to 80–90% of total cost
  • Land Leverage Up to 50% of land value
  • Max ARV Up to 70% of completed value
  • Draw Structure Inspection-based disbursements
  • Term 12–24 months
  • Interest Rate 10–13%
  • Origination 2–3 points
  • Loan Size $200,000 – $10,000,000

Ideal For

New construction SFR Infill development Small multifamily builds Teardown & rebuild ADU construction Spec homes

What We Look At

  • As-completed value Appraisal or comp basis
  • Construction budget Detailed cost breakdown required
  • Builder experience Licensed GC required
  • Entitlements & permits Must be in place or near
