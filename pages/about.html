About North Star Capital

For investors, by investors. Fast, common-sense private lending for real estate investors who get deals done.

Who We Are

North Star Capital is a private lender specializing in fix and flip loans, DSCR loans, and ground-up construction financing for real estate investors across the United States. We underwrite based on the asset — property value, ARV, and your exit strategy — not W-2s or credit scores.

We lend in 46 states with fast closings, typically 5–10 business days. Whether you're a seasoned investor scaling a portfolio or executing your first flip, we structure deals around your timeline.

Our Mission

Our mission is to give real estate investors access to fast, reliable private capital — so deals close on time, portfolios grow, and opportunities don't get missed. We built North Star Capital because we know what it costs to lose a deal waiting on a bank.
