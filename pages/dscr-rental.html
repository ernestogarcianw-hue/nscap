The Capital Behind Your Rental Portfolio.

Rental property investors think differently. You're focused on cash flow, equity build, and the long game. Our DSCR loans are built around that mindset — qualifying on the property's income potential, not your personal tax returns or employment history.

Whether you're executing the BRRRR strategy, building a single-family rental portfolio, or acquiring small multi-family, we underwrite on what matters — the asset's current value and income potential. Including short-term rentals — Airbnb and VRBO properties are considered.

How Our DSCR Loan Process Works

Whether you're acquiring a new rental or refinancing out of existing financing, the process is straightforward — we underwrite the asset, not your tax returns.

01

Submit Your Rental Deal

Share the property address, purchase price, current or projected rents, and your strategy. We respond with terms in 24 hours.

02

DSCR Review & Approval

We assess the property's value and income potential. No W-2s, no personal income docs — the asset qualifies itself.

03

Close & Start Cash-Flowing

Close in 10–15 business days. Your loan is a 30-year fixed or ARM — designed for the long hold, not a short-term bridge.

04

Hold, Refi, or Repeat

Hold long-term and collect rents, execute a cash-out refi when the equity is there, or use the BRRRR strategy to recycle capital into the next acquisition.
