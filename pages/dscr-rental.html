The Capital Bridge Between Deal and Portfolio.

Rental property investors move differently than flippers. You're thinking about cash flow, equity build, and the long game. Our DSCR loans are structured around that mindset — fast capital to acquire or stabilize a property, with a clear path to permanent financing.

Whether you're executing the BRRRR strategy, building a single-family rental portfolio, or acquiring a small multi-family, we bridge the gap between your purchase and your long-term refi. We underwrite on the property's income potential and current value — not your tax returns or employment history.

Vacant or tenanted, transitional or stabilized — if the numbers work on the asset, we can fund it.

How the BRRRR Bridge Process Works

A common use case: buy a rental property with bridge capital, rehab if needed, get it leased up, then refi into a 30-year DSCR loan to recycle your equity and do it again.

01

Submit Your Rental Deal

Share the property address, purchase price, current or projected rents, and your exit plan. We respond with terms in 24 hours.

02

DSCR Review & Approval

We assess the property's value and income potential. No W-2s, no personal income docs — the asset qualifies itself.

03

Fund & Stabilize

Close in 5–10 business days. Use the bridge period to renovate, lease up, or season rents — whatever your hold strategy requires.

04

Refi & Repeat

Once stabilized, refinance into a long-term DSCR loan. Pull equity back out. Repeat on the next deal.
